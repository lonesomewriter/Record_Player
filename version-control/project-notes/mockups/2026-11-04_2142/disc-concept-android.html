The disc is home. Library, Playlists, Settings, and Inbox arc down its right edge. Swipe right through Home → Library → Playlists → Settings, and left to the previous page. From Now Playing, a left swipe returns to wherever the song was last chosen (a playlist, album, artist or the Library); otherwise it returns Home. Export progress can be hidden and reopened from Inbox.
